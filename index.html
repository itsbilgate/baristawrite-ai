Writing task

Scenario: Incorrect milk in a latte A customer messages the café: “I ordered an oat-milk latte, but my drink was made with regular milk. I specifically asked for oat milk, and I’m upset.”

Your role: You are the barista replying to the customer.

  • Write 60–90 words.
  • Apologize politely and acknowledge the specific problem.
  • Explain how you will resolve it.
  • Use a professional, friendly tone. Do not blame the customer or a colleague.
